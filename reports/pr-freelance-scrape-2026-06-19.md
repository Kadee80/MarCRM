**Fractional/interim demand is real but hard to find publicly.** The strongest freelance PR opportunities this week came from direct agency outreach targets (Peregrine, Gregory) rather than job board postings. The market for senior freelance PR is growing — estimated 3X growth in fractional executive postings since 2018 — but posting behavior lags: companies fill senior freelance slots via networks before going public.

**Crypto/digital assets is the hottest contract PR vertical.** GSR Markets represents a pattern: well-funded, institutionally-minded crypto/digital asset firms are hiring contract comms professionals as their communications needs scale rapidly alongside capital raising and M&A activity. Mark's traditional financial PR background positions him well here.

**Financial services PR agencies are in a growth phase.** Both Peregrine (7+ open roles) and Gregory (just won PR Agency of the Year) show signs of rapid client growth. Agency growth = overflow work. Direct outreach to financial PR agencies positioning Mark as overflow capacity is the highest-ROI move this week.

**Rate environment:** Senior freelance PR in finance/fintech runs $100–$175/hr for contracts and $10K–$25K/month for retainers. Compensation text is rarely posted — all negotiation happens in the room.
