## Market Trends

**Fractional and interim legal demand is accelerating in 2026.** The fractional GC model has moved from novelty to standard practice for PE-backed portcos, emerging VC managers, and growth-stage companies. Platforms like Ontra, Paragon, and LOD are intermediating this demand at scale — and the quality of engagements available through them (top-tier financial institutions, major alternative asset managers) has risen materially. This is no longer overflow/doc review work; it's substantive, high-judgment counsel.

**Fund formation is the most active subvertical.** All three Grade A leads involve private funds or VC fund formation. Emerging manager launches are accelerating (driven by post-2025 PE activity and VC market recovery), and many Fund I / Fund II launches are happening with minimal in-house legal. The gap is precisely where a fractional fund counsel can step in.

**Securities/financial institution contracting is a secondary demand spike.** MSFTAs, ACAs, and structured product agreements are being staffed via ALSP channels (LOD, Axiom) at meaningful hourly rates ($100–150/hr) — a strong secondary track if fund formation pipeline softens.

**Remote is the default.** All 5 leads offer remote or remote-flex. The geography barrier for independent legal counsel is effectively gone in these verticals.
