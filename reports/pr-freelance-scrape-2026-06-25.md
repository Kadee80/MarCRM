The freelance PR/comms market continues to be dominated by aggregator and marketplace pages (Upwork, Toptal, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, FlexJobs, RemotePRJobs) rather than named, fresh, apply-now postings. The strongest in-sector opportunities remain (a) interim/contract leadership backfills at larger finance/B2B brands, and (b) direct agency-overflow / white-label business development — confirmed again by the dominance of stale-but-high-fit listings this week.
