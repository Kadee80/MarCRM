## Caveats on This Run

**Both freelance pipelines produced nothing.** Not one net-new lead in pr-freelance or legal-freelance. This wasn't for lack of searching — eight separate query angles were run across Indeed, CareerBuilder, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor, GoInhouse, fractionaljobs.io and Google-indexed LinkedIn. Every result was either already in the CRM or a generic board landing page.

**A scoring error worth knowing about.** LearnTastic was initially scored 76 with a perfect 50/50 intent, driven by a "posted within 72 hours" reading of a Jul 18 date on GoInhouse. The full-history dedup check revealed the role was already captured on 6/18 — the Jul 18 date was a re-syndication, not a new posting. This is a systematic risk with aggregator job boards: they surface refresh dates that look like post dates, which inflates the recency component of the intent score. Worth watching for in future runs, and an argument for weighting original-source postings over aggregators.

**Three pipelines produced zero qualifying leads:** ai-consulting, media, coaching-ops and legal-consulting. Searches returned market-level analysis and vendor listicles rather than company-specific trigger events. Rather than score speculative leads to fill the report, they're omitted.

**Net read:** this was a strong week for funding-trigger leads and a dead week for job-board leads. Six real leads is a thin but honest count — padding it would have meant re-importing companies already in the CRM.
