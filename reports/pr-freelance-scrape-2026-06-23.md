## Market Trends: PR Freelance (June 2026)

**Fractional PR demand is growing.** Platforms like Go Fractional and FractionalJobs.io are showing consistent listings for senior PR/growth roles at bootstrapped and early-stage companies — mostly consumer, SaaS, and services. The fractional model is normalizing for PR at $50–$95/hr entry and $150–$250/hr for senior B2B operators.

**Agency overflow is the best recurring revenue path.** Mission North and Crackle PR both operate with lean senior teams and use external senior contractors for capacity management. These relationships — once established — generate recurring project work without having to pitch each engagement.

**Financial/IR PR freelance is thin this week.** The specific finance/IR/PE-backed PR niche showed few open postings this run. Best leads in that sector came from staffing channels (Scion, Robert Half) and evergreen agency calls (Crackle), not specific job board posts. The CrowdPharm/Hot Iron Health IR consultant role that surfaced in search was removed in December 2025.

**Fintech-specific PR agencies are growing their senior consultant networks.** Crackle PR's fintech case study (Creditsafe) and their active senior hiring are a signal that B2B fintech PR work is healthy. Agencies with this specialization are a natural target for Mark's inbound pitch.
