## Trends in the legal-freelance market (this week)

The fractional/interim/contract legal market is now dominated by a stable roster of ALSPs and staffing firms (Axiom, Latitude, MLA, Ropes & Gray's hourly funds bench, Ontra, Robert Half, Epiq Counsel, Tower Legal, Paragon, Legal.io, Hire an Esquire, Lawtrades, Priori). Fund-formation and private-funds contract work remains active but is concentrated in already-captured channels (Ropes & Gray, MLA, Tower, Ontra, Allocate, aVenture).
