## Trends in PR Freelance Market (this week)

1. **Fractional/interim is a productized service line now.** Forthright and other boutiques sell "interim communications director" as a packaged offering — they need senior benches, which means freelance opportunities live one layer below the surface (not on public job boards).
2. **Agency overflow remains the dominant freelance vector.** No mid-large agency surveyed lists freelance roles publicly with any regularity, but every conversation with talent leads at firms like 5WPR and CSG returns to project overflow as the operating reality.
3. **Vested / Caliber partnership is structurally the most accessible door.** They're the only U.S. financial-services PR firm with a formal contractor pool (VCo System) AND an explicit current freelancer posting. Hit Vested first, then ride the partnership into Caliber.
4. **LinkedIn job-search results dwarf actual contract postings.** A keyword search for "freelance public relations" surfaces tens of thousands of results, but the freelance signal-to-noise ratio inside indexed postings is poor. Direct firm targeting beats board scraping in this vertical.
