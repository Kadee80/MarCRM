## Market trends — legal freelance (week of 2026-06-08)

The fractional-GC model continues to harden as a standard offering, not a niche: providers consistently quote ~$3,000–$8,000/month for part-time outsourced legal leadership (a stated 75–85% saving vs. a $250k–$400k+ full-time GC), which keeps pulling demand down-market into earlier-stage and mid-sized companies. On the supply side, the channel is consolidating around a familiar set of marketplaces and benches (Axiom, Ontra, LAWCLERK, TraverseGC, Paragon, Major Lindsey & Africa) plus recruiters like Taylor Root — which is exactly why net-new named reqs are now thin in a daily scrape.

Two structural notes worth tracking. First, MLA's 2026 interim snapshot and regional commentary suggest interim hiring in some Northeast markets (e.g., Boston) is rotating away from generic commercial-contracts attorneys toward boutique finance, life-sciences and health-care work, with some contract attorneys reverting to full-time — a signal to lead with specialist fund/finance positioning rather than generalist contracts. Second, embedded fractional-GC firms (Next Era Legal, Intellectual Strategies, Northstar Legal) are scaling their own benches, which is both competition and a partnering/overflow opportunity for a senior fractional counsel.
