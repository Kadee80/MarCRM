## Lead 1 — Early-Stage Investment Firm, Fractional Chief Compliance Officer

**Grade A · Combined 90 (Fit 50 / Intent 40) · Urgency 65**
**Remote (US) · $95-$155/hr · 5-20 hrs/week · Posted 2026-07-13**
**Source:** [Go Fractional](https://www.gofractional.com/job/fractional-chief-compliance-officer-cmrb15e7)

### Background

An early-stage, fully remote investment firm founded by technology entrepreneurs moving into financial services. They are launching their first fund and need someone to build the compliance and regulatory foundation from zero: assess current posture, produce a prioritized regulatory roadmap, run SEC and FINRA registration and ongoing filings, select and manage auditors, write internal policies and controls, and get the fund audit-ready as it scales.

### Why it matters for Katie

This is the emerging-manager buyer profile almost verbatim. Fund I, no internal legal or compliance bench, enough complexity to need senior judgment, nowhere near enough volume to justify a full-time hire — so they are explicitly buying 5-20 hours a week of it. The stated rate is credible and unambiguous, which removes the usual guesswork.

The most telling line in the posting is the requirement to "translate complex regulatory requirements into clear, actionable guidance for a non-finance founding team." That is a judgment sale, not an execution sale, and it is the part of the work that is hardest to commoditize.

### The honest caveat

**This is posted as a CCO role, not a counsel role.** It asks for Series 65 / CFA / CAMS-type certifications and audit experience, which Katie may not hold. It scored 50 on fit because the underlying work — fund structure, registration mechanics, fund documentation, governance, policy drafting — is squarely her ICP, not because she is a literal match for the title. Worth being clear-eyed about that before investing time.

### Pitch angle

Do not apply as a compliance officer.

Approach as **fund counsel who can own the legal and regulatory build for a Fund I launch**: entity and fund structuring, registration mechanics, LPA / PPM / subscription document review, governance and policy drafting. Then either propose a scoped counsel engagement running alongside a credentialed CCO, or ask directly whether they would consider splitting the mandate.

Emerging managers routinely post one blended role because they have not yet worked out that they need two. Naming that distinction for them is itself a demonstration of the judgment they are trying to buy.

Even if this particular engagement does not land, a first-time fund founded by non-finance operators is close to the ideal long-term fractional fund-counsel client. Worth the relationship regardless of outcome.

**Action:** verify the posting is live, then apply via Go Fractional with the scoped-counsel reframe rather than a straight application.

## Lead 2 — Media/PR Monitoring SaaS, Interim Head of Legal (Fractional)

**Grade B · Combined 60 (Fit 20 / Intent 40) · Urgency 75**
**Remote (US) · $25/hr listed — see below · 40 hrs/week · Posted 2026-07-13**
**Source:** [Go Fractional](https://www.gofractional.com/job/interim-head-of-legal-fractional-cmrb2mqa)

### Background

A global SaaS platform serving media, PR, and journalism — media monitoring, journalist relationship management, story distribution. Mid-size, roughly up to 500 employees. They want an interim Head of Legal to own commercial legal end to end: enterprise customer agreements through a CLM, vendor and procurement contracts, AI and data/content licensing terms, light employment counsel, plus managing one contract hire. Reports to the COO/CFO.

### Why it matters for Katie

Commercial contracts and vendor/customer paper are core ICP workstreams, and this is real GC-level scope rather than document review dressed up. The urgency is genuine — "step in soon," "time-sensitive environment," "operate autonomously from day one," with month- and quarter-end deal spikes.

### Two things to verify before spending any time

1. **The rate is almost certainly wrong.** $25/hr for a 40-hour-a-week GC-level role is not market-credible, and every comparable interim Head of Legal on the same board lists $130-$300/hr. This scored zero on comp credibility. Confirm the real number before investing effort — if it genuinely is $25/hr, drop it.

2. **"Fractional" is a misnomer here.** Forty hours a week is full-time interim coverage, not a part-time retainer. It would crowd out everything else. That is a portfolio-strategy question, not just a calendar one.

### Fit limits

No fund angle whatsoever. The AI / data / content licensing emphasis and the light employment counsel piece both sit outside Katie's core corporate and fund strength.

### Pitch angle

If the rate checks out: lead with high-volume enterprise commercial contracting and deal-desk throughput under quarter-end pressure, plus governance and vendor paper. Be candid that AI and content licensing is adjacent rather than core — overclaiming there is easy to catch.

The stated preference for prior acquisition exposure is the real hook. M&A cleanup and post-acquisition contract harmonization is squarely her background and is worth leading with alongside the contracting volume.

**Action:** message via Go Fractional to confirm actual rate and true hours commitment before applying.

## Market trends

**The fractional legal market is internationalizing faster than the US segment is growing.** Two thirds of Go Fractional's current legal listings are UK/EU. Interim Head of Legal and interim senior counsel roles on 6-to-12-month fixed terms are now standard practice in London, Amsterdam, and Paris in a way they are not yet in the US. US demand is still concentrated in startup fractional GC work rather than structured interim cover.

**Rate benchmarks are consolidating.** Interim and fractional senior legal roles are clustering at $130-$225/hr, with fractional GC at established companies reaching $200-$300/hr. ZipRecruiter's fractional legal counsel average sits at $144k annualized. Katie should be anchoring at the upper half of that band, not the middle.

**"Contract" continues to mean two incompatible things.** Tower Legal and Robert Half postings are temp W-2 staffing at $80-120/hr with no client relationship. Go Fractional and Latitude engagements are true independent-operator arrangements at $130-200/hr with direct client contact. Same word, materially different business. The `employmentTypeRaw` versus `engagementModel` split in the CRM is doing useful work here and is worth keeping clean.

**ALSP market context.** The alternative legal services market has reached roughly $28.5B, growing at about 11.7% CAGR, with 57% of corporate law departments already using ALSPs. The structural tailwind is real — the constraint on this pipeline is source coverage, not market demand.
