## Trends in the Legal Freelance Market (week of 2026-06-15)

- **VC financing and fund formation remain active workstreams** for interim/contract counsel, consistent with prior PR-freelance findings about elevated fund-formation activity (per the [[project_freelance_icp_enhancement]] ICP).
- **Comp for senior contract corporate attorneys is strong** — $160K-$320K annualized equivalents across the three Latitude listings, reinforcing that this market pays well for Big Law/in-house pedigree even on a contract basis.
- **Recency is hard to verify** for staffing-firm "open opportunities" pages — these often persist as evergreen listings rather than dated postings, which suppressed intent-score recency points across the board.
