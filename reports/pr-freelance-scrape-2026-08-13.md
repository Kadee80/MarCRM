## Trends in the PR freelance market

The freelance/fractional PR market remains hot and well-priced for senior operators: ZipRecruiter puts the average freelance PR consultant at ~$135K/yr (most $150K–$170K) and contract PR at ~$84K/yr as of early August 2026. Fractional comms retainers cluster around $1,500–$3,000/mo plus per-placement bonuses for part-time (15–20 hrs/mo) 1099 arrangements. The global PR services market is projected at ~$107B in 2026 (5.3% CAGR to 2034), with agencies increasingly leaning on white-label/overflow partners to absorb "lumpy" client demand rather than hiring senior specialists full-time — structurally favorable for Mark's model. Fintech/crypto and IR/IPO comms continue to generate the most senior, well-compensated contract demand.
