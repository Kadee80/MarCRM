## Trends in the PR freelance market (this run)

- **Fractional/retainer comms is now a standing category, not a niche.** The recurring pattern across postings: **$1,500–$3,000/month base retainer + per-placement bonuses ($250–$1,000/outlet)** for fractional comms directors at small/lean companies (e.g., the Neolytix-style model). Finance-sector retainers run far higher ($10k–$30k/month for full fintech/IR PR programs). Mark can position at the senior end of the retainer market.
- **Rate bimodality is stark.** Fresh postings this week ranged from **$40–50/hr** (B2B SaaS direct hire) to **$120–130/hr** (senior contract at a pre-IPO consumer brand). Screening on rate alone is misleading — scope and buyer sophistication matter more.
- **Mediabistro went independent (Jan 1, 2026)** and is upgrading freelance/project profile features; a rising share of its best postings are contract/freelance senior roles. Worth adding as a recurring source to monitor.
- **Pre-IPO / IPO-readiness comms demand is real** (IR narrative, executive visibility, liquidity-event messaging) — a core Mark lane — but most of it flows through named search firms already in the CRM (Hanson Search, Go Fractional, interim-exec shops) rather than fresh direct postings.
