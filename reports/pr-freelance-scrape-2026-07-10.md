## Market trends (this run)

- **Interim/fractional is now a productized recruiting category.** Comms Search & Selection, Hanson Search, GigX, and Chameleon Collective are all marketing curated freelance comms benches with fast-deployment promises — the buyer increasingly hires the bench, not a single posting. Being *on* these benches is the game.
- **Finance-comms specialization is deepening.** IR, capital-markets narrative, and corporate-affairs practices are being called out explicitly by recruiters (Comms Search's IR discipline; efinancialcareers investment-comms boards). Mark's finance angle is a differentiator, not a nice-to-have.
- **Recency is getting hard to read.** Many of the strongest channels are evergreen service/marketplace pages rather than dated postings, which suppresses the recency component of the intent score even for high-value leads. Worth weighting registerable benches on fit + urgency signals rather than post date.
- **Employment-type ambiguity persists.** Several "consultant"/"contract" hits are W-2 in-house or temp-staffing roles (e.g., the excluded Fidelity role), not freelance. The employmentTypeRaw vs. engagementModel split is doing real filtering work.
