## Trends in the legal freelance market

Fractional GC rates for corporate/commercial work are holding at the high end — $150-350/hr on the operating-company side (Fractionus $250-350, OneRail $150-200), well above pure staffing rates ($70-120/hr at LOD and Tower). The spread confirms the strategic split in this pipeline: **operator-model fractional GC engagements pay 2-3x the ALSP staffing rate** for comparable subject matter.

Marketplaces (Go Fractional, Axiom, Lawtrades, Peerpoint, LOD) remain the dominant top-of-funnel — most credible fractional/interim legal demand routes through them rather than direct company postings. Private-funds contract demand stays steady out of NY staffing firms (Tower, Broughton Group, Interlink, Lateral Link all showing funds roles), mostly on-site or hybrid NY, with occasional remote NY-barred variants.
