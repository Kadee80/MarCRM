## Trends in the PR freelance market

**The volume this week was real, the fit was not.** Aggregator boards returned plenty of results for the search clusters, but the yield after filtering was near zero, and the reasons are structural rather than seasonal:

1. **"Fractional" has been captured by finance and ops.** ZipRecruiter's `Fractional Communications` query returns overwhelmingly fractional Controllers, CFOs, CTOs and bookkeepers — roles matching on the word "communication" appearing in a soft-skills bullet. ZipRecruiter's own breakdown of that query is 87% full-time and only 1% contract, which tells you the keyword no longer isolates genuine fractional comms work. Boolean clusters built on "fractional" alone are decaying as a discovery method.

2. **The specialist boards were quiet, not empty.** The Work Crowd posted nothing new in PR and comms during the window — its two most recent PR briefs (15 and 16 July) were both captured in the 20 July run. The PR Net's `Freelance` basis filter returned "No entries available," and its unfiltered board is currently junior agency-side roles: account coordinators, account executives, one communications assistant. Mediabistro's PR board told the same story — nine listings, all agency AE/AC level, none freelance or senior. Go Fractional added no comms or PR roles in the window across either its main board or its marketing category.

3. **The senior fractional comms roles that do exist are being posted, filled and closed fast.** Three of the most promising results this week were dead on verification: the Hanson Search freelance Senior Account Manager (London, £275–300/day, "available almost immediately") had already closed applications, and two BuiltIn/Bou freelance PR listings were removed in 2024 and 2025 respectively but still rank in search. Indexed search results are lagging the actual market by a meaningful margin, which argues for going direct to source boards over relying on search discovery.

4. **The adjacent-role trap is widening.** Several roles matched on sector and engagement model but had no PR remit at all: a fintech/B2B SaaS fractional marketing director (demand gen), a fractional marketing lead, a fractional community/SEO content writer. Separately, 59 Pines' "Capital Formation & Investor Relations Rep" — flexible on fractional or contract, up to $300K — is a capital-raising role that requires working a personal network of accredited investors to source and close them, not an investor-communications remit. Sector-first searching without a hard PR/comms gate produces a lot of noise; the 2-bucket title test earned its keep this week.

5. **Where the demand actually sits.** The one qualifying lead is an agency buying senior fractional capacity, which is consistent with the pattern across recent runs: agencies and boutiques with overflow are more reliable buyers than operating companies, because they already have budget, clients and a delivery team and are only missing senior judgement.
