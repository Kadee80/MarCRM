The two top-scoring leads (c/side, FlexingIt) are both content-strategy contracts where the real value is earned-media and executive-visibility skill — Mark's differentiator is bundling strategy with execution, which most platform writers can't.

**Fractional/interim is now an institutionalized service line, not just a posting type.** Firms like Forthright Advising (and previously captured Chameleon Collective, Go Fractional, Daybook) sell interim/fractional comms directors as a product. The repeatable play is registering on these platforms/firms as overflow capacity rather than chasing one-off listings.

Senior strategic comms commands well above the platform floor, reinforcing that Mark should lead with strategy, not compete on hourly content rates.

**Finance/IR freelance demand still concentrates in marketplaces** (Toptal, Fintalent, The Work Crowd) and established IR/comms firms (captured in prior runs: KCSA, The Equity Group, Joele Frank, Brunswick). Few net-new finance-sector *postings* this week — the channel is relationship/registration, not job-board.
