## Trends in the legal freelance market

The fractional/interim GC market continues to be framed by providers as a "right-sized" alternative to a $250k–$400k+ full-time GC, with fractional engagements pitched at roughly $3k–$8k/month and increasingly remote or hybrid. This run reinforced two patterns: (1) **fund-formation work is moving to contract/hourly delivery** — both aVenture (an operating fintech hiring fractional fund counsel directly) and Tower Legal (a staffing firm running several concurrent private-funds contract roles at $80–120/hr) show fund-document work being unbundled into freelance engagements; and (2) **interim commercial/SaaS-contracts demand is "immediate-need" driven** — MLA's interim desk is placing remote SaaS counsel at $90–95/hr to bridge bandwidth gaps. Staffing firms / ALSPs (Tower, MLA, Latitude, Robert Half, Taylor Root) remain the highest-volume channel; the most differentiated leads are operating companies hiring fractional fund/GC capacity directly.
