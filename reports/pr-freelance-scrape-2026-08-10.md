## Trends in the PR freelance market

- **Rates hold up at the senior end.** ZipRecruiter pegs the average freelance PR consultant at ~$135k/yr (most $150k–$170k), while general remote PR averages ~$84k — reinforcing that Mark's strategic/senior positioning commands a premium over generalist contract work.
- **Fractional comms is now a deliberate strategy, not a stopgap.** Fractional/interim demand is concentrated in growth-stage, PE/VC-backed and mid-market companies; fintech is a repeatedly named regulated vertical. Typical structures: $1,500–$3,000/mo base retainers with per-placement bonuses, 15–20 hrs/mo, 3-month pilots that convert.
- **White-label/overflow is a structural channel.** Agencies increasingly treat white-label execution as the default way to absorb overflow without turning away clients — validating Mark's overflow-partner positioning with firms like Caliber and Vested.
- **Aggregator noise is rising.** A growing share of "freelance PR" search real estate is directory/SEO pages rather than live reqs, which is compressing the volume of directly-actionable postings surfacing per scrape. Expect leaner runs and lean harder on agency-target BD.
