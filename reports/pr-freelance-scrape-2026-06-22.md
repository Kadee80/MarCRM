## Market Trends (PR Freelance — June 2026)

**Fractional and contractor PR demand is rising.** The fractional executive market has grown 3X since 2018, and senior PR professionals are increasingly part of this trend. More companies are hiring fractional communications leaders rather than full-time Directors, particularly PE/VC-backed companies and scaling B2B SaaS businesses.

**Agency overflow is a structural opportunity.** PR agencies (particularly remote-first shops like Lightspeed, LaunchSquad, and Mission North) are building freelance benches to handle client surges without headcount. Mark should be on multiple agency benches simultaneously.

**B2B SaaS is the hottest sector for senior PR contractors.** Companies like Torry Harris — scaling B2B SaaS platforms with PR agency retainers but limited internal comms capacity — represent the clearest path to well-paid, flexible work.

**Finance/fintech PR specialists command premium rates.** The Work Crowd's 2026 rate guide shows US communications freelancers in fintech/financial services earning significantly above general PR market rates. Caliber's Director role at $120-150k FTE signals strong demand for this specialization.

**"Contract-to-hire" is the new trial period.** Agencies like LaunchSquad are using 3-6 month W-2 contractor stints as paid auditions. For Mark, this is a lower-commitment way to join an agency roster with an upgrade path.
