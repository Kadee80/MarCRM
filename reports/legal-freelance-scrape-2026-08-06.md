## Trends in the legal freelance market

- **The fractional-GC channel is consolidating onto curated marketplaces.** Go Fractional and Fractional Jobs are now the highest-signal sources for genuinely new, well-scoped fractional counsel mandates — better yield than Indeed/ZipRecruiter aggregator pages, which mostly return evergreen SEO and salary-benchmark content.
- **Rates are holding at $150–$260/hr** for US fractional GC / senior counsel engagements (AE Studio $150–200, FAR.AI $155–215, Bush & Bush $150–225, NextGrad $200–260 before it closed). Compensation credibility is rarely the blocker; engagement-model clarity (interim vs. converting seat) is.
- **AI-sector demand is a throughline** — AE Studio, FAR.AI, and multiple closed postings were AI/data-science orgs needing contracts, IP/open-source, and governance help. This is a repeatable positioning wedge for Katie.
- **Fund-formation freelance supply is dominated by already-captured ALSPs/staffing** (Axiom, Latitude, Allocate, MLA, Larson Maddox, Epiq). Net-new fund-specific *companies* were scarce this week; the incremental value is in tracking new requisitions from those existing accounts.
- **Law firms are emerging as fractional-GC buyers** (Bush & Bush) — a distinct buyerType worth watching: firms scaling multi-state need their own governance/compliance GC.
