## Trends Spotted

**Cybersecurity is the standout category two days running.** Neo ($100M) and Empirical Security ($25M) both raised on Jul 20 on the same underlying thesis: AI is rewriting the enterprise risk model, and existing tooling doesn't cover autonomous software. Two well-funded companies chasing the same narrative in the same week is a differentiation fight — and differentiation fights are when companies buy comms.

**Capital is rotating to the "AI control layer."** The Jul 20 tape clustered hard around infrastructure, security, payments rails and regulated workflow rather than application-layer AI. For pr-marketing targeting, that means the best-fit companies now are the ones selling into a bottleneck, not the ones with the flashiest demo.

**More strategic/PE money, fewer classic priced rounds.** Quorum (Enlightenment Capital), Wagmo (Curql) and HALO (Agilent) were all strategic investments with undisclosed amounts. These are worth tracking but score lower on intent — quiet deals don't create launch moments, and undisclosed size means the budget signal is unconfirmed.

**Freelance job boards produced nothing new for the second consecutive cycle.** Every qualifying posting was already in the CRM. Worth noting as a pattern, not just a bad day — see Next Steps.
