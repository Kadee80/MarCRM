## PR Freelance Market Trends (June 2026)

**Fractional is mainstream.** Fractional executive placements grew 300% since 2018 per Revelio Labs (2025 data), with comms and marketing roles leading. The "fractional CCO" concept has gone from niche to expected for VC-backed companies under Series B.

**Agency overflow demand is up.** Multiple agencies contacted via search are explicitly seeking freelance overflow talent — HKA, BarkleyOKRP, BPM-PR, and Mission North are all running talent network or direct freelance hiring strategies rather than FTE-only approaches.

**AI is a qualifier, not a disqualifier.** Employers expect candidates to be conversant with AI-assisted media monitoring (Cision, Meltwater) and draft generation tools. Senior freelancers who demonstrate AI fluency while positioning their judgment as irreplaceable are most competitive.

**Tech PR still hot, quantum computing emerging.** B2B tech and deep tech (quantum, climate, AI infrastructure) are now active PR spend categories. HKA's quantum tech opening is a leading indicator of where agency specialty demand is moving.

**Remote standard in senior freelance.** Across the leads found, 5 of 8 are either remote or flexible. Location requirements are loosening even at Director level for contract engagements.
