## Trends Spotted

**Fractional Jobs and Go Fractional posted five legal roles in a single day (Aug 17).** All five new legal-freelance leads today came from just two boards, and four of the five were posted within the 72-hour window. These two boards are consistently outperforming the ALSP/staffing sources for freshness.

**Geographic gating is the recurring blocker, not fit.** Three of five legal leads today are Canada-only, UK-only or effectively onsite-Bay-Area. The engagement models are exactly right; the geography is not. Worth adding a US-eligibility pre-filter to the scrape to stop burning slots on unreachable roles.

**Fit scores in legal-freelance are structurally capped at 20–30.** Every legal lead today lost the same two sub-scores: no fund/private-funds work (−20) and, usually, no stated comp (−10). The pipeline is filling with corporate/commercial contract work, not the fund-formation work that scores highest. Either the fund-specific supply has genuinely dried up on these boards, or the search clusters need re-weighting toward fund-specific sources (Ontra, Latitude, Larson Maddox, Tower Legal) that have produced those leads historically.

**PR freelance yield was thin.** Indeed, ZipRecruiter, The PR Net, The Work Crowd and Mediabistro all returned listings already captured in prior reports. Only one genuinely new pr-freelance lead surfaced, and it came from Remote Rocketship — a source not currently in the standard rotation.
