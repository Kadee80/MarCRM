## Market Trends

The legal freelance market in 2026 continues to bifurcate along two tracks: **platform-mediated freelance** (Ontra, Axiom Law, Latitude Legal) and **direct interim placements** via staffing firms (MLA) or company direct-hire. Key trends:

- **Fund formation freelance is growing**: Ontra and Allocate both reflect strong demand from asset managers who need fractional fund counsel without the cost of in-house hires. Emerging manager activity (Fund I/II launches) is driving overflow demand.
- **Commercial contracts remains the highest-volume slot**: MLA, Latitude, and Axiom all show persistent demand for remote contract attorneys with commercial paper experience — this is the most liquid segment of the legal freelance market.
- **ALSP platforms are the dominant sourcing channel**: Ontra, Axiom, and Latitude together account for the bulk of credentialed remote legal work. Registering as an attorney on all three platforms gives maximum pipeline coverage.
- **Compensation compression in startup direct-hire**: aVenture's $80-120k listing exemplifies a pattern of startups trying to hire senior attorneys at below-market rates by framing the role as equity-upside. Worth engaging but negotiate hard.
