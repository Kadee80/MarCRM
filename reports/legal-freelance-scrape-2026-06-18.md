## Market Trends

**Fractional GC demand accelerating.** The legal freelance market showed continued volume across all segments this week. Fractional GC boards (FractionalPulse, ACC Jobline) are seeing steady flow of SMB and mid-market operating company postings, typically 1–2 days/week on retainer. These are lower-comp but flexible — good for building a portfolio.

**Hedge fund/family office consulting premium.** Platforms like Arootah represent the high end: $400–600/hr, no day-to-day presence required, pure advisory. The catch is seniority — these platforms want former GCs with direct HF/FO tenure. Getting on one of these rosters (Arootah, Bridgewater's advisory alumni network, similar) is a multi-year positioning play.

**ALSPs (Axiom, Latitude) still dominant.** Both already captured in prior scrapes. No material new openings from these platforms this cycle beyond the Latitude 6420 Corporate Counsel role (SaaS/tech vendor contracts, ~$150–170K annualized, W-2 — not prioritized this cycle given existing Latitude coverage).

**Emerging market signal.** New platforms like FractionalPulse are aggregating non-C-suite fractional legal roles (CLO, Chief Legal Counsel). Currently low-comp, but shows that early-stage and growth companies are increasingly looking for legal leadership as a fractional buy. Worth monitoring for when comp bands move up.
