For finance-sector reach, The Work Crowd remains the most ICP-relevant network to keep monitoring.
- **Fractional comms is real but thin on finance.** Fractional/interim communications hiring is clearly growing (Go Fractional, Forthright Advising, Chameleon fractional CCO), but this week's live fractional inventory skewed to Finance/Engineering/Sales; comms-specific fractional briefs were scarce and mostly nonprofit/foundation rather than finance.
- **Blockchain/crypto PR retainers stay hot.** Crypto/Web3 PR is running on $8K–$25K/mo agency retainers — a segment where a senior white-label/overflow operator could plug into agencies, worth a dedicated cluster next run.
- **Contract ≠ freelance.** Much "contract" comms inventory on LinkedIn/Indeed is temp W-2 staffing (e.g., 12-month IR maternity-cover style roles), not true freelance/retainer. Keep tracking employmentTypeRaw vs. engagementModel separately.
