## Market Trends This Week

The fractional GC / contract counsel market remains structurally hot, but the supply side is consolidating around three lanes:

1. **Platform/network plays** (Ontra, Peerpoint, Axiom, Go Fractional, Unified Law, Latitude) — onboard once, then receive routed engagements. Best for Katie's part-time availability because the platforms absorb client sourcing.
2. **ALSP/staffing temp engagements** (Tower Legal, Robert Half, Major Lindsey, Lateral Link) — project-based fund-formation, fund document, and corporate paper work at $80–250/hr depending on practice area. Steady, lower variance, but lower ceiling.
3. **Direct operating-company fractional GC** (Sleep Doctor, HungryPanda, TodayTix, Indigo) — fractional GC roles with explicit path to FTE if the relationship grows. Higher per-engagement intensity but more strategic ownership.

Fund formation continues to be the single most defensible specialty for Katie. Side letters, feeder funds, and emerging-manager LPAs remain in high demand — Tower's posting, Ropes & Gray's funds attorney engagement (already captured), and Ontra's network are all anchored on this workstream. The week's strongest single application target is **Tower Legal Solutions** because it stacks pure-ICP scope with a single onboarding round that unlocks adjacent private-funds engagements.

The healthtech/DME compliance posting (Sleep Doctor) is a reminder that fractional GC demand keeps spreading into regulated verticals where in-house cost is hard to justify but compliance program build is non-optional. Katie should treat these as "stretch but valuable" rather than primary targets.
