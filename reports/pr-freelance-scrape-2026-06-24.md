## Trends in the PR freelance market

- **Aggregator saturation.** In-sector named postings are increasingly buried under marketplace/aggregator pages (Upwork, Toptal, FlexJobs, ZipRecruiter). Direct-to-agency outreach is becoming the higher-yield channel for senior freelance work.
- **Fractional is mainstream.** Fractional/interim communications placements continue to grow (reportedly ~300% since 2018), with average fractional comms pay around the low-$90k/yr equivalent. Demand is strongest in tech, finance, and PE-backed companies.
