## Legal Freelance Market Trends

The market continues to split into two lanes. The first is *individual live reqs* — genuine one-off freelance/interim/fractional postings (Ontra, The Mom Project, the Fractional Jobs startup), which remain relatively scarce on open boards and are the highest-intent finds when they appear. The second is *enrollment channels* — marketplaces and flexible-talent firms (Priori, Hire an Esquire, Paragon, plus previously captured Go Fractional, Lawtrades, Peerpoint, Elevate, Axiom, Allocate, Epiq Counsel) that convert a single onboarding into recurring routed work. For a fund/corporate specialist, the highest ROI is stacking onto several complementary channels — a fund-formation platform (Ontra), a corporate/funds project marketplace (Priori), and an embedded/interim placement firm (Paragon) — so briefs flow continuously rather than depending on catching dated posts.

Fractional-GC comp benchmarks held steady this cycle: roughly $3,000–$8,000/month for retainer fractional GC engagements, and an average fractional-legal-counsel figure around $144k/year (most $118k–$172k) per current board data — useful anchors when Katie sets rates on the marketplaces.
