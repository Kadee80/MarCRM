## Market Trends

The flexible-legal market this week skews toward network/bench buyers over discrete postings. The strongest fund-formation demand is now intermediated by platforms (Ontra, Allocate-type marketplaces) and ALSPs (Paragon, Axiom, Epiq Counsel) rather than direct fund hires — meaning the fastest path to recurring fund work is getting onto two or three reputable benches rather than chasing one-off listings. Fractional-GC pricing continues to anchor around $3,000–$8,000/month for part-time outsourced leadership (roughly 75–85% below full-time GC cost), which keeps demand structurally strong among emerging managers and portfolio companies. Distinguish engagement models carefully: Ontra and boutique-firm overflow are true 1099 operator work, while Axiom and Paragon are W-2/managed placements — both useful, but different on rate, autonomy, and tax treatment.
