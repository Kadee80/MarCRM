## Trends in the legal freelance market

The fractional/interim GC model continues to broaden past tech startups into consumer, healthcare-adjacent, and operating companies — CookUnity hiring an embedded fractional counsel specifically for a B2B sales motion is a good example of demand for narrowly-scoped, deal-speed commercial counsel rather than a full GC. On the supply side, ALSPs and legal-talent networks (Legalpeople, Atlas Road, t\|e General Counsel this week) remain the highest-leverage pipeline: a single bench relationship converts into recurring corporate/M&A/securities placements. Fund-formation and private-funds demand is real and steady (large remote counts on Indeed/ZipRecruiter), but genuinely *freelance/interim* fund seats are scarce inside any 7-day window and cluster at the larger firms and asset managers already captured (Allocate, Ropes & Gray, aVenture). Published comp remains the exception, which keeps most intent scores capped on the comp-credibility bucket.
